## Building

### Rush · the brain of your bar
Data management applied to food service. Rush is automated management software for premium bars: invoices, recipes, staff and cash register data come together in one place, and an AI assistant turns them into decisions. It gives bar and restaurant owners a concrete way to put AI to work in their business, every day.

Running with our first pilot client, and a waiting list of venues across Italy ready to go live at launch.
